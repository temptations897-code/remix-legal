Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Remix ("Remix", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use Remix through Discord.

1. Information We May Collect

Remix may process limited information necessary to operate its features.

This may include:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server/Guild IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs where required
  • Discord Role IDs where required
  • Music track titles
  • Track identifiers
  • Track artists/authors
  • Track duration
  • Track URLs or source identifiers
  • Artwork URLs
  • Saved playlists
  • Favorites
  • Music-related preferences
  • Server configuration

2. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Remix is designed to minimise data collection.

We do not intentionally collect or permanently store:

  • Discord passwords
  • Discord authentication tokens
  • Payment card information
  • Audio recordings of users
  • Files from your computer
  • Your Discord account password
  • Private Discord messages unrelated to Remix functionality

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide music playback functionality.
  • Maintain queues and playback sessions.
  • Save user preferences.
  • Store favorites and playlists.
  • Provide music history features.
  • Maintain server configuration.
  • Improve reliability and performance.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Prevent abuse and protect the service.
  • Maintain security.
  • Respond to support requests.

4. Music Data

When you search for or play music using Remix, information associated with the requested track may be processed.

This may include the track title, artist, duration, identifier, source URL and artwork.

Music data may be processed through third-party music or audio services required to provide playback.

5. Database Storage

Where persistent storage is required, Remix may use a database service such as MongoDB.

Only information necessary for the relevant feature should be stored.

Remix does not use its database as a permanent storage location for music or audio recordings.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality, maintain security, comply with applicable requirements or resolve disputes.

Certain information may be automatically removed after a period of inactivity or according to configured retention limits.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers where necessary to operate Remix.

These may include:

  • Discord
  • Hosting providers
  • Database providers
  • Lavalink infrastructure
  • Music or audio providers
  • Security or monitoring services where applicable

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Discord

Remix operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is separately governed by Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Remix is not responsible for information collected directly by Discord or other third-party services.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed by Remix.

However, no online service, database, network or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Deletion

You may request deletion of information associated with your use of Remix.

Requests can be submitted through the official Remix support channels.

We may need to retain limited information where required for security, legal, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution purposes.

11. Children and Age Requirements

Remix is provided through Discord and is subject to Discord's own age requirements and policies.

We do not intentionally request or collect unnecessary personal information from children.

12. International Data Processing

Because Remix may use infrastructure and third-party providers located in different countries, information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence.

Where applicable, reasonable steps are taken to ensure that data processing is carried out in accordance with relevant data-protection requirements.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include:

  • Requesting access to certain information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion of information.
  • Objecting to or restricting certain processing.

Privacy requests can be submitted through the official Remix support channels.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Remix changes, new features are introduced or legal requirements change.

The "Last Updated" date will be changed when material updates are made.

15. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please use the official Remix support channels.

16. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective from 1 October 2026.
